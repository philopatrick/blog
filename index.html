About / Position

Humanities
& systems.

Technology should become a quiet scaffold for human flourishing—not another object demanding attention.

I am interested in the human condition and in the structures that make thought possible.

“思考的内容物是独立于思考的表达物的……精神的外化是非常重要的，世界的精神等价物便是语言。简单也许是一种高级。”

Philosophy, literature, consciousness, distributed systems, memory architecture: to me these are not separate territories. They meet wherever an inner life depends on an outer form.

I care about finite working memory, linear flow, tactile permanence, and tools that disappear into use. This site is a public notebook for ideas that have survived long enough to deserve a stable address.
