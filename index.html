About me

P

An intellectual of the humanities and systems — exploring the human condition, quiet tools, and the scaffolds of thought.

“思考的内容物是独立于思考的表达物的……精神的外化是非常重要的，世界的精神等价物便是语言。简单也许是一种高级。”

To me, technology is never an end in itself; it is an externalized scaffold for human flourishing, memory, and reflection. I work at the intersection of humanistic curiosity and systems engineering, fascinated by how external structures shape our inner life.

Living with finite working memory, I resist noisy interruptions, dopamine-trapping algorithms, and bloated interfaces. I believe deeply in quiet tools, linear flow, and tactile permanence.

This site is a calm notebook in public: a home for crystallized thoughts, reading digests, and architectural observations that deserve more space than a fleeting thought.
